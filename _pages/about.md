I'm an incoming PhD student in computer science at the University of Toronto, where I'll be working on computational imaging of astrophysical and geophysical phenomena, and more generally **ML for Science**. I completed my undergrad in mathematics and MEng at UofT as well, where I explored [computational models of synaptic plasticity](https://sites.google.com/view/lnsbsp/home) at the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, wrote about [exotic spheres](https://pnphan.github.io/seniorthesis.pdf) at the Department of Mathematics, [modelled hurricanes on giant planets](https://storms.physics.utoronto.ca/) at the Department of Physics, and [analyzed trends in the tropopause](https://hydrogroup.civmin.utoronto.ca/) using radiosonde data at the Faculty of Engineering.
