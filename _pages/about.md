I'm an incoming PhD student in computer science at the University of Toronto, where I'll be working on computational imaging of astrophysical and geophysical phenomena, and more generally **Computational Imaging and ML for Science**. I completed my undergrad in mathematics and MEng at UofT as well, where I explored [computational models of synaptic plasticity](https://sites.google.com/view/lnsbsp/home) at the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, wrote about [exotic spheres](https://pnphan.github.io/seniorthesis.pdf) at the Department of Mathematics, [modelled hurricanes on giant planets](https://storms.physics.utoronto.ca/) at the Department of Physics, [developed hyperspectral image super-resolution techniques](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~lindell/teaching/2529/past_projects/2024/report/peter-phan.pdf) at the Department of Computer Science, and [analyzed trends in the tropopause](https://hydrogroup.civmin.utoronto.ca/) using radiosonde data at the Faculty of Engineering. Along the way, I served as a (Head) TA for over 15 iterations of various mathematics courses, became the only undergraduate finalist in history for the university-wide [TA Teaching Excellence Award](https://tatp.utoronto.ca/awards/), and dabbled in various internal pedagogy projects at the Institute for the Study of University Pedagogy and as a [Jackman Scholar](https://www.humanities.utoronto.ca/sir/about-sir/previous-projects).
