## Background

For the Fall 2025 application cycle, I applied to 2 PhD programs in computer science:

- UofT (Accepted)
- MIT (Waitlisted)

UofT has around 50-75 spots and 700-1000 applicants, and MIT has around 150 spots, 4000+ applicants, and under 100 people on the waitlist.


My background is non-standard; I did my bachelor's in mathematics and master's in mechanical/industral engineering, but along the way I gained experience in a variety of topics that converged to what I'm working on now: ML/AI for Science. I had no experience in ML until June 2024, and submitted my applications in December 2024. 




## Other Useful Resources
- [CSRankings](https://csrankings.org/) - Helps identify top research programs.
- [GradCafe](https://www.thegradcafe.com/) - See application results and discussions.
- Faculty and lab websites of your target programs.

This guide is a general roadmap; always check specific program requirements for details. Good luck!
