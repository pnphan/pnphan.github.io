For the Fall 2025 application cycle, I applied to 2 PhD programs in computer science: UofT and MIT. I received an offer from UofT and was placed on the waitlist at MIT. My background is non-standard; I did my bachelor's in mathematics and master's in mechanical/industral engineering. Along the way, I learnt a variety of subjects that converged to the topics I'm now working on: I've had experiences in computational neuroscience, educational research, pure mathematics, atmospheric and climate science, and computational imaging. To an outsider, my journey seems erratic: I'm often asked how I managed to do such "different" things, all somewhat successfully. This is the wrong question to ask, but it's only a manifestation of the assumptions on which this question is based on; assumptions that I believe to be deeply wrong and detrimental to one's intellectual journey.
