## Background

For the Fall 2025 application cycle, I applied to 2 PhD programs in computer science:

- UofT (Accepted)
- MIT (Waitlisted)

UofT has around 50-75 spots for 700-1000 applicants, and MIT has around 150 spots, 4000+ applicants, and under 100 people on the waitlist.

I did a bachelor's in mathematics and master's in mechanical/industral engineering, but along the way I gained experience in a variety of topics that converged to what I'm working on now: ML/AI for Science. I had no knowledge of ML until June 2024, completed only two CS courses during my bachelor's (CSC108 and CSC363), and submitted my applications in December 2024. 

This post is an attempt of gathering everything I've learnt through this process. My path is an outlier, but I've learned a lot of things during this process that I think are broadly applicable.

## The Application

The application generally consists of your CV, 3 reference letters, and a statement of purpose. Some schools also require a personal statement, but none that I applied to. Application deadlines are generally in early December, but late reference letters are sometimes accepted with no guarantees they'll actually be viewed. 


## Other Useful Resources
- [CSRankings](https://csrankings.org/) - Helps identify top research programs.
- [GradCafe](https://www.thegradcafe.com/) - See application results and discussions.
- Faculty and lab websites of your target programs.
